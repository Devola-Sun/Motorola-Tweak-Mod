## What's Changed in v2.5.6

This release reorganizes the in-app settings, adds optional Liquid Glass styling for the lock-screen clock and Moto Widgets, and introduces Russian and Simplified Chinese language support.

### Settings & Navigation
- Reorganized settings into clearer categories, with dedicated Lock Screen and Moto Widgets pages.
- Added restart actions within settings sections to make applying changes easier.
- Improved back navigation from individual settings pages.

### Liquid Glass
- **Lock-screen clock:** Added an optional Liquid Glass effect with translucent gradients and reflective edges on supported clock digits. The effect works independently of clock size and restores stock rendering in always-on display.
- **Moto Widgets:** Added optional Liquid Glass styling for supported home-screen widget elements, including the clock, date, weather, temperature, and city information, without changing their size or layout.
- Both options are disabled by default and can be configured independently. These are visual shading effects, not actual wallpaper refraction or blur; unsupported elements retain their stock appearance.

### Languages
- Added **Russian** and **Simplified Chinese** to the in-app language selector, alongside Japanese and English.

### Additional Improvements
- Added lock-screen clock enlargement controls and an optional right-edge swipe gesture to open the lock-screen camera.
- Added build-time verification of the Xposed module identity to prevent incorrect preference loading.

### Installation & Updating
1. Install the attached APK over your existing version.
2. Keep the required LSPosed scopes enabled and retain root permissions for restart actions.
3. Restart SystemUI after changing lock-screen options. For Moto Widgets, use **Restart Moto Widgets and launcher to apply**; the Moto Launcher LSPosed scope (`com.motorola.launcher3`) is required.

Clock and widget compatibility depends on the device ROM and selected style. Visual effects and transitions still require on-device verification. If an option causes issues, disable it and restart the corresponding process.
