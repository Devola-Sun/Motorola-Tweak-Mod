## What's Changed in v2.5.4

This release brings refined visuals to the Control Center (Quick Settings), enhancements to Lock Screen shortcuts, and overall SystemUI hook stability improvements.

### 🎛️ Control Center UI Refinements
* **Polished Quick Settings Layout:** Refined tile spacing, padding, and alignments across both folded and unfolded screen modes.
* **Enhanced Visual Balance:** Improved background blur and translucent accent handling for a cleaner, modern look.
* **Responsive Interactions:** Smoother visual feedback during panel expansion and tile toggling.

### 🔒 Lock Screen Shortcut Enhancements
* **Improved Customization & Reliability:** Optimized shortcut launch responsiveness and touch hitboxes on the lock screen.
* **Visual Consistency:** Cleaned up icon sizing, padding, and alignment to seamlessly integrate with custom themes and screen densities.

### 🛠️ General Stability & Bug Fixes
* **SystemUI Hook Optimization:** Hardened hook injection points against timing issues and edge-case NPEs across configuration changes (fold/unfold).
* **Preferences Sync Reliability:** Further solidified settings synchronization between the module settings app and SystemUI background processes.

---

### 📦 Installation & Updating
1. Install the updated APK (`app-debug.apk` or `app-release.apk`) over your existing version.
2. Ensure **MockingBat** retains Superuser access in **KernelSU** / **APatch** / **Magisk**.
3. Perform one **Soft Reboot** to bind updated hooks.
4. Open the MockingBat app to configure or re-apply settings, then restart SystemUI/Launcher if prompted.
